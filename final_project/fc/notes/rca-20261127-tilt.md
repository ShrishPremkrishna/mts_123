# RCA 2026-11-27: imu_tilt lie-down after service (mts / blue)

**Verdict:** this is a sensor fault, not a mount offset, a real lean or a guard bug. The BNO085's calibrated accelerometer now reads **+3.05 m/s² high on chip X**. Our filter and the chip's rotation vector both trust that accel, so both report the same false ~10°. That is why `rv_tilt_diff` stays under 2°.

## Pipeline
1. **Driver** (v3 patch): reads accel 0x01, gyro 0x02 and the rotation vector 0x05. With `FC_IMU_FILTER=1` (the stock default), our Mahony estimate replaces the chip quaternion. It converges to the accel direction (kp 2/s).
2. **Mount:** the URDF `imu_sensor` pitch is −2.35619. The hardware interface publishes q·offset⁻¹, and gyro and accel = Ry(−135°)·chip.
3. **Consumers:**
   - the walker's projected gravity = R⁻¹(0,0,−1), with no calibration term;
   - the bridge's `ImuHealth` tilt = acos(1−2(x²+y²)). It trips at more than `bad_tilt_deg` for 2 s at zero command, then the bridge waits 0.75 s and runs lie-down → R3.
   - Nothing in our stack adds an offset. The filter's ki bias can shift tilt by at most ~b/kp ≈ 2°.

## Evidence
**Today**, level on the stand with legs off, 20 s read-only capture:
- published accel (−2.10, −0.34, 12.04), so |a| = **12.22**;
- chip frame (**9.99**, −0.34, −7.03), σ 0.015;
- tilt 10.7°.

**Nov 23–24**, same stand pose, 5 recordings:
- chip frame (**6.66**, −0.14, −7.09), |a| = 9.73;
- tilt 2.0°; standing with motors holding also read 2.0°;
- floor runs on Nov 24–25 had a median tilt of 2–4°.

**Why it is not a rotation:** only chip X changed, and a rotation (remounted or replaced board) preserves |a|. Correcting X alone to |a| = 9.81 gives 2.1°, which is the old value.

**Other observations:**
- robot.log prints only "Correct Product ID", so a chip swap can be neither confirmed nor excluded.
- Gyro freezes are much worse: 18k of 30k samples frozen during homing, longest 71 s, against 0 at rest on Nov 24. The chip is degraded.
- Standing read **22°** against 10–11° sitting. Probably the walker leaning against the false gravity; unverified.

**Guard path:**
- The override **did apply**: the bridge passes `ImuHealth({})`. The latch reasons say "tilt 16.1" (bad 16) and "12.1" (warn 12), and the trip came at 22°.
- `estop.json` `tilt_deg` 9.61 is FallenGuard's tilt **when the latch was written, after lowering**.
- `imu_params.json` vanished because `stage.sh` runs `rsync --delete`. **Fixed:** `--exclude /imu_params.json`.

## Likely mechanism (unverified)
One of two causes:
- A bad accel dynamic calibration auto-saved to flash (DCD). BNO08x chips learn offsets while handled, and the saved offsets survive power cycles.
- Accelerometer damage during service.

**Decisive test (stack stopped):** `imu/bno_reader.py` reads raw accel 0x14 and can run `clear_dcd_and_reset()`. If clearing fixes it, the cause was the DCD; if raw 0x14 shows the offset too, it is hardware.

## Fix (v4, installed on mts Nov 27; tilt 0.26°)
**Driver** `~/fc-v1/build/imu-filter-v4/install/.../libcontrol_board_hardware_interface.so`, sha256 `f849f2547da29bdc…69779c6`; patch `fc/robot/imu/stock-imu-filter.patch` (v1–v4).
- New `rt/fc_imu_cal.hpp` loads `accel_bias_chip` at start from `$FC_IMU_CAL` or `~/fc-v1/run/imu_cal.json`.
- It subtracts the bias from every 0x01 sample, both for the filter and in the published accel. So the walker and the tilt guards see corrected gravity.
- It logs `FC_IMU_CAL … APPLIED`. A missing or invalid file means zero bias (v3 behaviour).
- `colcon test`: 3/3 pass. The new `test_fc_imu_cal` takes the measured vector from 10.0° to 0.01°, and a real 8° pitch still reads 8°.

**`fcctl.sh imu-level [--dry-run] [--legs-on] | check | show`** (`imu/imu_level.py`):
- Robot level on the stand, legs off.
- Captures 10 s and refuses on motion, noise, outliers, a stall marker, more than 30° tilt or a bias above 6 m/s².
- Accounts for the bias already applied.
- Saves `run/imu_cal.json` outside the rsync'd tree and keeps `.prev`.
- **Already written** from today's capture: (3.0515, −0.3359, −0.0946).

**Guards and tests:**
- `ready` now checks tilt at rest after standing and before the bridge. Above 6° it fails with `imu` (exit 5) and a message to "run imu-level"; the robot is left standing, and `--skip-imu-check` overrides.
- `status.imu_cal` shows stored against applied.
- `tests/test_imu_level.py`: 13 pass. The new fcctl tests need Linux and are **unrun**.
- The app/ctl op was not done.

```
~/fc-v1/robot/fcctl.sh start stock --confirm --speed-cap 0.80
grep FC_IMU_CAL ~/fc-v1/runs/<new>/robot.log     # APPLIED
~/fc-v1/robot/fcctl.sh imu-level check           # expect < 2 deg
rm ~/fc-v1/robot/imu_params.json                 # back to 10/6 deg
```
- **Rollback:** relink v3. A stale `imu_cal.json` is harmless under v3.
- **On the floor:** standing should read 2–4°.
- **Re-run `imu-level`** after any DCD clear or chip swap.
