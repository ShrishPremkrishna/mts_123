# Blue startup investigation · November 20, 2026

## The failed Home

Blue ran source `0a479c1` with the redundant IMU field profile. Its operator confirmed the load-bearing stand and requested Home only from Chrome. The UI reported failure 20.45 s after Home began. The native driver spent 15.004 s waiting before ever enabling the drives. At its deadline the effective known ages of the primary orientation and gyro were 11.56 and 5.07 ms. Both were inside the 100 ms limit. The terminal board receipt recorded zero Homes and one fault. No walker activation or leg motion command followed. The owning manager exited. Camera, detector and recorder survived until an explicit manager-first cleanup verified the session stopped. Physical motor torque was not independently instrumented. [Original log, receipts and hashes](../../outputs/pupper-fc-intercept/verification/physical-home/first-home-and-boot.json)

The error merged two predicates. The primary had to deliver three continuous seconds of fresh balance samples, gravity varying by at most 1°, peak angular speed at most 0.05 rad/s and mean rate at most 0.025 rad/s. The second predicate required 0.5 s of uninterrupted agreement with the same chip's alternate orientation and gyro reports. The 15 s was the outer deadline. The old failure record contained neither earlier primary decisions nor alternate measurements. It cannot identify the particular predicate responsible. Six starts do not establish a field failure rate. The previously recorded physical shutdown with an undiagnosed timeout and the later camera-clearance/arming failure are separate incidents.

On this same Blue, four motor-free cold sensor resets qualified both predicates in 3.105–3.109 s. The backup first qualified in 0.599–0.609 s. In two more cold resets the actual camera, Hailo detector and MCAP recorder started within 1.05 ms of one another. Both predicates passed in 3.108 and 3.113 s. Both runs reported 10 Hz detector output with no IMU resets or alternate sequence gaps. One quiet run later measured a 0.0836 rad/s excursion, sufficient to restart a stationary window. It occurred after qualification. Persistent bad calibration, permanent missing alternate reports and camera contention did not reproduce across these trials. | Camera, detector and recorder | Sensor/load comparisons did not reproduce the failure. Both groups returned 20 full transfers and 19 nonempty, checksum-valid replies. The old Home loop could continue seeking a missing stop. | Require verified motor-board responses before enable and through mechanical travel. Bound seeking and return using each enrolled joint's travel, speed and contact threshold. Reject invalid feedback and clear incomplete offsets. No calibrated absolute-position check is applied to an unhomed joint. |

The 609.8 s historical launch-to-first-body-request included a measured 5.08 s mechanical Home and a later 252 s interval before walker activation. Connect, sensor qualification and mechanical travel measure different endpoints. An apples-to-apples time saved through physical first motion needs a supervised standing and play measurement.

## Independent audits and failure recovery

Exact Claude Opus 5.5 and Fable 5.2 separately reviewed the same frozen source and de-identified incident from fresh directories with tools disabled. Neither received the chat, another review or the later Pi comparisons. Both returned complete responses after 362.29 and 259.70 s. Both identified ambiguous telemetry, the misplaced backup qualification, missing mechanical checks and the risk of blindly retrying. The final changes were tested separately. One concern about an unbounded standing acknowledgment was resolved against the omitted gateway implementation, which has a four-second deadline and a separate Stop acknowledgment. The current operator path also checks the live hardware component before declaring Home. [Opus review](../../outputs/pupper-fc-intercept/verification/physical-home/opus-5-5-blind.md) · [Fable review](../../outputs/pupper-fc-intercept/verification/physical-home/fable-5-2-blind.md) · [Gateway](../hardware/software_start.py)

The driver buffers a bounded trace covering both IMU report pairs. A refused startup preserves it after sending neutral. A normal successful Home avoids the disk write. A positively identified pre-enable refusal reaches the dashboard immediately, cleans up the recorded owner and offers Prepare after verified process exit. Ambiguous failures after motion retain their evidence and require inspection. The operator must explicitly request another Home. The elevated Wi-Fi preference on this Pi can be reverted independently using the UUID and previous priority stored in `~/.local/state/pupper-fc/wifi-priority-before.json`.
